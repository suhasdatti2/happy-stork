Since 1953, one address on the causeway has stayed loyal to the same idea — good drinks, low light and a door that never seems to close.

'53SINCE
The Origin

Born on the 79th Street Causeway.

Happy's opened in 1953, back when North Bay Village was a string of supper clubs and the bridge to Miami Beach buzzed every night of the week. It was built as two things at once — a lounge for the ones who wanted to stay, and a liquor store for the ones who needed one more for the road.

Owners, decades and a few coats of paint have come and gone. What never changed is the feeling: walk in once and you're a stranger; walk in twice and you're a regular.
