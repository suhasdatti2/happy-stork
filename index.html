Our Story

A Miami original,
poured since 1953.

Long before the skyline glittered the way it does now, Happy's opened its doors on the 79th Street Causeway — half lounge, half liquor store, all heart. Seven decades later we're still here: the same low light, the same honest pours, the same crowd of regulars who made this their living room.

"Not a trend. A tradition."
