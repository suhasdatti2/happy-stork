## How to add them (do it yourself, no credits)

1. On your computer, rename each photo to the names above (lowercase, `.jpg`).
2. Go to **github.com/suhasdatti2/happy-stork**
3. Branch dropdown → pick **`claude/beautiful-gauss-s929dr`**
4. Open the **`assets`** → **`img`** folder
5. **`Add file ▾` → `Upload files`** → drag your photos in → **Commit changes**

## Tips
- **Format:** `.jpg` works best. (PNG/WEBP also fine if you rename the slot.)
- **Size:** ~1000–1400px on the long edge keeps the page fast.
- **Shape:** slides are portrait (4:5). Vertical phone photos fit best; landscape
  photos center-crop.
- **Captions:** want different wording on a slide? Just ask and I'll change it.
